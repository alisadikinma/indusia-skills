| Quick Win (0-6 bln) | SIRI-AI diagnostic (digitization roadmap data-driven) | LKH Precicon | Naikkan nilai jasa konsultasi |
| Strategic Bet (6-24 bln) | NBO recommendation engine (B2B) | ElectGo / EMD | Monetisasi traffic digital |
