# WS6 — Narrative Retention Framework Deep Report

## Executive Summary

**Problem this framework solves:** How to structure a 60-240 second video so a busy Tionghoa-Batam SME owner (Pak Indra archetype, practical-skeptic register, ROI-numerate) does not bounce in the first 3 seconds AND remembers the promise at the end strongly enough to act (DM, schedule discovery call, request proposal).

**The 5-Beat scaffold (HOOK → Foreshadow → BODY → Peak → CTA)** is the load-bearing container on which all WS1 video-viewer psychology is mounted. WS1 told us *what* persuasion levers exist (curiosity, social proof, narrative transportation, Peak-End rule). WS6 tells us *where in the timeline* to deploy them.

**Five headline findings:**

1. **Curiosity-gap (Loewenstein 1994) is the only HOOK mechanism with peer-reviewed empirical foundation** — every "3-second rule" platform prescription (TikTok, YouTube) is downstream of this cognitive primitive
2. **Peak-End Rule (Kahneman 1993, Do/Rupert/Wolford 2008, Heath bros 2017) determines remembered impression** — viewers retain (peak intensity + final moment), not average experience. Therefore Peak placement at 70-85% runtime + CTA in the last 10-15% is non-negotiable
3. **Foreshadow / setup-payoff (Pixar Rule #7, Truby Symbol Web, Sorkin Intention-Obstacle) is what holds attention through BODY** — without a planted promise to be paid off later, retention collapses at the 30-second algorithmic cliff
4. **CTA placement timing matters more than CTA copy** — Wistia 36k-CTA study + HubSpot 330k-CTA study converge: middle-placed CTAs out-convert end-only for videos >5min, while ≤5min videos suffer if CTA arrives before Peak. In-video CTAs +380% vs sidebar (HubSpot)
5. **Indonesian platform behavior (44.9 hrs/month TikTok per Indonesian, 29 hrs YouTube, 81% YouTube adoption, Gen Z 48% 1-5hr/day, 7/10 dual-screen)** validates **vertical-first short-form for top-of-funnel** and **horizontal/long-form YouTube for mid-funnel B2B promo** — confirms the format-routing decision already locked in `04-video-format-decision.md`

## Section 1 — Why a 5-Beat (Not 3-Act, Not 15-Beat)

### The compression rationale

McKee (Source #6) and Truby (#9) provide the canonical 3-act / 22-step structures — designed for 90-120 minute feature films. Snyder's Save the Cat 15-beat sheet (#7) is the most compressible Hollywood structure, designed for 100-minute screenplays. None of these are operational for 60-240 second B2B promo without violent compression.

**The 5-beat scaffold is the minimum viable narrative architecture** that still preserves:
- A psychologically valid Hook (Loewenstein gap)
- A setup-payoff loop (Pixar Rule #7, Truby Symbol Web compressed to a single planted detail)
- A pacing engine through BODY (Sorkin's Intention-Obstacle drive shaft, Zeigarnik open loops)
- A measured Peak (Heath bros' "Elevation" engineered, not stumbled into)
- A timed CTA (Wistia / HubSpot empirical timing)

Save the Cat's "Theme Stated" beat (~5% mark of a screenplay) maps cleanly to our Foreshadow position (10-25% of runtime for short-form). The 5-beat is a compression, not a contradiction, of the 15-beat canon.

### Why not the 3-beat TikTok prescription (Hook-Body-Close)?

TikTok For Business (#5) officially prescribes "Hook → Body → Close". For 15-60 second purely-organic TikTok content this is correct — there's no time for a discrete Foreshadow beat. But for **B2B promotional content** running 60-240 seconds with a paying-customer conversion goal, dropping Foreshadow costs retention through the 30-60 second window. The 5-beat is the B2B operational specialization.

## Section 2 — HOOK Beat (0-10% runtime)

### Mechanism — Curiosity-Gap (Loewenstein 1994, Source #1)

Loewenstein's "information gap" theory of curiosity (Psychological Bulletin 1994, 2300+ citations) defines curiosity as a state of cognitive deprivation: the gap between *what you know* and *what you feel you should know*. When the gap is small and the perceived importance high, curiosity intensity peaks.

Golman & Loewenstein 2015 (#2) formalize this — curiosity utility scales with **(perceived importance × confidence in resolvability)**. Translation for video hooks:

> **A hook works when:** the viewer perceives the missing information as personally important + believes the video will resolve it.

### Operational rules

| Rule | Source | Application |
|---|---|---|
| **First 3 seconds critical** | TikTok BP (#5), YouTube Key Moments (#17) | Visual + audio + curiosity-gap question delivered before 3-sec mark |
| **30-second algorithmic cliff** | YouTube Key Moments (#17) | If retention drops below platform threshold at 30s, distribution penalized |
| **Specific > vague** | Loewenstein gap intensity formula | "Anda tahu jam berapa truk Anda berangkat tadi pagi?" beats "Apakah Anda tau kondisi fleet Anda?" |
| **Owner-pov direct address** | TikTok BP, Sorkin Intention-Obstacle (#11) | "Pak Indra, Anda tahu..." beats "Para pengusaha trucking sering..." |
| **Pattern interrupt visual** | Zeigarnik (#15) — open-loop tension | First frame is unexpected: HP getar bukan office desk; container blank space bukan operasi normal |

### Indonesian Tionghoa Batam owner specificity

WS1 Indonesian low-arousal register finding applies here: Pak Indra does NOT respond to shouty-hyperactive hook. Calm direct address with specific data lands better than "🚨 STOP SCROLLING 🚨" energy.

**Validated hook pattern library** (already integrated into `02-archetype-routing-hooks.md` Addendum): 7 owner-visibility-gap curiosity-gap hooks, one per gap.

### Anti-patterns

- ❌ Hook = brand intro ("Hai, kami INDUSIA Kecerdasan Digital...")
- ❌ Hook = generic problem ("Banyak owner trucking di Batam mengalami...")
- ❌ Hook = aspirational ("Bayangkan masa depan logistik...")
- ❌ Hook = data dump without gap framing ("Industri logistik Indonesia tumbuh 8%...")

## Section 3 — FORESHADOW Beat (10-25% runtime)

### Mechanism — Setup-Payoff Loop

Pixar Rule #7 (Coats 2011, Source #8) compresses Chekhov's principle into one line: *"Come up with your ending first, then work backward."* The Foreshadow beat plants the resolution before the BODY mechanism is even revealed — creating Zeigarnik open-loop tension (Source #15) that holds viewer through the middle.

Truby's "Symbol Web" (#9) generalizes this: an effective short narrative weaves 1-3 motifs / objects / phrases through Hook → Foreshadow → BODY → Peak. Each weave-point reinforces memory anchoring.

Green & Brock 2000 (#16) provide the empirical anchor: narrative transportation (viewer immersion in story) lowers critical-counter-arguing. The Foreshadow beat is where transportation either takes hold or fails — if the viewer doesn't trust the story is going somewhere, they bounce.

### Operational rules

| Rule | Source | Application |
|---|---|---|
| **Plant the Peak before revealing mechanism** | Pixar Rule #7, Snyder Theme-Stated beat (#7) | Foreshadow drops a hint of what owner will see in Peak ("Pak Indra punya cara baru lihat ini...") |
| **Open a sub-loop** | Zeigarnik (#15) | Promise an unresolved thread that BODY will close ("Bayangkan kalau Bapak bisa tanya ke siapa aja...") |
| **Owner-hero framing** | Vogler Writer's Journey (#10) — Stages 1-4 | Position owner as the protagonist; the AI / system is the mentor-tool, not the hero |
| **Conflict articulated** | Sorkin Intention-Obstacle (#11) | "Owner wants X, obstacle is Y" — make the BODY a battle, not a feature tour |

### Mapping to 5-beat runtime

| Format | Foreshadow window |
|---|---|
| Vertical 60s | 6-15s |
| Horizontal 120s | 12-30s |
| Long-form B2B 240s | 24-60s |
| Pitch video clip 75s | 7-19s |

### Anti-patterns

- ❌ Foreshadow = feature list ("Sistem ini punya GPS, RFID, AI...")
- ❌ Foreshadow = mechanism explanation upfront (kills BODY tension)
- ❌ No Foreshadow at all — straight Hook to feature dump
- ❌ Foreshadow contradicts Peak (planted detail doesn't actually pay off)

## Section 4 — BODY Beat (25-70% runtime)

### Mechanism — Pacing + Drive Shaft + Open Loops

McKee (#6) defines a **beat** as "the smallest unit of conflict change". BODY of a video promo is a sequence of micro-beats: each one shifts viewer state (curiosity → understanding → recognition → anticipation → emotional setup for Peak).

Truby's steps 9-13 (#9) — Plan / Opponent's Plan / Drive / Attack / Apparent Defeat — provide the BODY arc. For B2B promo, **the "attack" is the demo** (Asisten WhatsApp screen, dashboard glance, fuel-fraud detection sequence) and **"apparent defeat" is the implicit conflict** (owner used to be blind, now sees).

Sorkin's Intention-Obstacle (#11) is the drive shaft of every micro-beat: each scene must show owner wanting something, something blocking, owner-via-system overcoming. Without this engine, BODY becomes a feature tour and viewer drops.

Zeigarnik (#15) — sub-loops opened in Foreshadow MUST be progressively closed (or deliberately held open for Peak/CTA). If sub-loops accumulate without resolution, viewer feels lost. If all close before Peak, no tension remains.

### Operational rules

| Rule | Source | Application |
|---|---|---|
| **Triangulate to 3-pillar** | Internal LOCK-1 | AI / IoT / Mobile mechanism shown in BODY, NOT enumerated |
| **Mandatory Asisten demo scene** | LOCK-3 + `04-video-format-decision.md` | ≥1 scene shows WhatsApp UI rendering Asisten interaction — 8-15 sec runtime in BODY |
| **Owner reaction shots** | Heath bros Power of Moments (#14) — Insight beat | After Asisten reveal, cut to Pak Indra reaction face — vindication, surprise, quiet confidence |
| **Concrete data on screen** | TikTok BP visual prescription | "B-5678 fuel anomali 15 L jam 9:15" beats abstract "fraud detected" |
| **Beat-to-beat conflict escalation** | Sorkin (#11), McKee (#6) | Each micro-beat ratchets stakes — first solar reveal, then fleet-wide picture, then financial implication |

### Mapping micro-beats to runtime

Short-form (60s vertical):
- 15-25s: Asisten reveal (visual)
- 25-32s: Demo interaction
- 32-42s: Implication scaling (one truck → fleet → margin)

Long-form (240s B2B):
- 60-100s: Asisten reveal + first demo
- 100-140s: Second demo (different gap)
- 140-168s: Implication scaling + comparable (vs. Eddi competitor without naming)

### Anti-patterns

- ❌ BODY = static feature list narrated
- ❌ BODY = single scene (no beat structure)
- ❌ BODY > 75% runtime (squeezes Peak + CTA out)
- ❌ No 3-pillar triangulation — feature mentioned without anchoring to pillar
- ❌ Missing Asisten demo scene (LOCK-3 violation)

## Section 5 — PEAK Beat (70-85% runtime)

### Mechanism — Peak-End Rule (Kahneman 1993, Do et al. 2008, Heath bros 2017)

Kahneman et al. 1993 (#12) — original Peak-End Rule experiment using colonoscopy patients — established that **retrospective evaluation of an experience is dominated by (peak intensity + end moment)**, not average. Duration is neglected.

Do, Rupert, Wolford 2008 (#13) replicated for **positive** experiences (DVDs, gifts) — confirming the rule applies to feel-good moments, not just pain. Critically validates that a well-engineered Peak in a promo video drives remembered impression even if BODY was unmemorable.

Heath & Heath 2017 (#14) operationalize: Peaks are engineered through **Elevation** (above-the-everyday quality), **Insight** (rewiring understanding), **Pride** (recognition moment), **Connection** (shared experience). For B2B owner promo, the canonical Peak design = **Insight + Pride** (owner sees ops in new way, feels in-control again).

### Operational rules

| Rule | Source | Application |
|---|---|---|
| **Place Peak at 70-85% runtime** | Kahneman + Heath bros + algorithmic retention norm | Earlier = post-peak deflation hurts CTA; later = no room for CTA |
| **Engineer Insight moment** | Heath bros (#14) | Owner says (or face shows) realization: *"Saya bisa lihat semua dari HP saya"* |
| **Single emotional moment, not multiple** | Kahneman peak-singularity | One Peak, not three "almost-peaks" — averaging effect kills memorability |
| **Sensory specificity** | Truby Symbol Web (#9) | Recall via specific concrete image (HP getar at magrib; gold ETA dashboard) |
| **Don't over-explain Peak** | Pixar Rule #4 Story Spine (#8) | Let visual + Pak Indra reaction carry it; voiceover light or absent |

### Mapping Peak to runtime

| Format | Peak window |
|---|---|
| Vertical 60s | 42-51s |
| Horizontal 120s | 84-102s |
| Long-form B2B 240s | 168-204s |
| Pitch video clip 75s | 53-64s |

### Anti-patterns

- ❌ Peak = feature crescendo (10 features named rapidly)
- ❌ Multiple Peaks (averaged out)
- ❌ Peak too early (50%) — deflation
- ❌ No reaction shot — viewer doesn't know to feel anything
- ❌ Heavy voiceover at Peak — kills emotional moment

## Section 6 — CTA Beat (85-100% runtime)

### Mechanism — Empirical Timing + Personalization

Wistia 36k-CTA study (#18) is the only large-N empirical analysis of video-specific CTA placement: **middle-placed CTAs out-convert end-only for videos >5 min**; ≤5 min videos = end CTA OK. Direct prescription for the 60-240 sec promo: end-CTA is correct.

HubSpot 330k-CTA study (#19) — broader CTA performance: **personalized CTAs +202%**, **in-video CTAs +380% vs sidebar**, single-CTA pages +32% vs multi-CTA.

TikTok For Business (#5) confirms CTA card lift: +45% recall, +65% brand affinity when on-screen.

### Operational rules

| Rule | Source | Application |
|---|---|---|
| **CTA in last 10-15% of runtime** | Wistia (#18) | For ≤5 min B2B promo |
| **In-video CTA, not separate landing** | HubSpot +380% (#19) | Show CTA copy + visual + tap-zone within video frame |
| **Single CTA, not multi** | HubSpot single-CTA +32% (#19) | "DM kami untuk demo Asisten — gratis, 30 menit." NOT "DM atau email atau visit website..." |
| **Soft consultative for B2B Tionghoa** | LOCK-4 + WS1 register | "DM untuk diskusi" beats "BELI SEKARANG!" |
| **CTA register-matched to audience** | Internal `04-video-format-decision.md` Addendum CTA table | Owner Tionghoa = soft; procurement = formal RFP path; sopir recruitment = direct + face-saving |

### CTA copy library (per audience)

| Audience | CTA Copy |
|---|---|
| Pak Indra owner Tionghoa | *"Pak, DM kami untuk demo Asisten — gratis, 30 menit. Tidak ada paksaan."* |
| Procurement / IT manager | *"Request proposal di [URL]"* |
| Sopir / karyawan (recruitment video) | *"Daftar di [link], kami panggil interview"* |
| Customer (tracking-app promo) | *"Scan QR, dapatkan link tracking"* |

### Anti-patterns

- ❌ Hard-sell CTA in Tionghoa SME register
- ❌ Multiple CTAs ("DM atau email atau call atau...")
- ❌ CTA before Peak (deflation effect)
- ❌ CTA cut off at end (last 5% — algorithmic risk if viewer doesn't reach)
- ❌ CTA copy without next-step clarity ("Hubungi kami!" — *bagaimana?*)
