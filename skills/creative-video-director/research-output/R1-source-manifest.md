### Sources Rejected (with reason)

- **Random Medium posts on B2B psychology** — No author credentials; AI-generated style; rejected per anti-blog-farm constraint
- **Wikipedia entries (Cialdini, Sutherland, GoTo)** — Useful for orientation but not authority-grade; substituted with primary publisher pages
- **HubSpot top-of-funnel listicles** — High pagerank but low research depth; only kept the Wistia-collab analysis (#19) which carries Wistia's data
- **Scribd-hosted PDFs of Edelman report** — Used official edelman.com URL instead
- **getlatka SaaS list** — Directory listing, no analytical content
- **General "Top X video marketing stats" aggregator blogs (digitalapplied, blondish, wix)** — Recycled Wyzowl data, no original research

### Coverage Gaps Identified

1. **Indonesian video promo / agency case studies** — Sparse academic coverage of B2B video specifically in Indonesian market. Largely covered via Tokopedia/GoTo proxy + Mekari positioning.
2. **Cannes Lions 2022-2023 B2B winners** — Cannes only launched Creative B2B as standalone category in 2022; 2022/2023 case teardowns less indexed than 2024/2025. Contagious archive #22 partially covers historical context.
3. **Bain B2B research direct PDF** — Co-authored 95:5 research with LinkedIn but Bain doesn't host a standalone publicly downloadable version; LinkedIn B2B Institute page (#8) is the primary distribution channel.
4. **Owner-operator / family-business buyer psychology specifically in SE Asia logistics** — Closest proxy is Indonesian SME digital adoption literature (Cat 5). Domain depth lives in `pakar-logistik-batam` skill, not academic literature.
