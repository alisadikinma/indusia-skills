## Top 5 Strategic Sources

1. **Loewenstein 1994 (#1)** — The peer-reviewed cognitive foundation of every Hook. If only one paper survives, this one explains the *mechanism*.
2. **Kahneman et al. 1993 + Heath bros 2017 (#12 + #14)** — Pair: theory (Peak-End Rule) + applied prescription (Power of Moments). Anchors the Peak beat with both rigor and recipe.
3. **Save the Cat 15-Beat Sheet (#7)** — Most operationally compressible structure for 3-4 min B2B promo; Theme-Stated beat = clean foreshadow slot.
4. **YouTube Key Moments doc + Wistia 36k CTA study (#17 + #18)** — First-party platform truth on retention signal definitions + the only large-N empirical study on CTA placement timing.
5. **TikTok For Business Creative Best Practices (#5) + Green-Brock Narrative Transportation (#16)** — Platform-level hook prescription + peer-reviewed mechanism of why story-format video bypasses critical thinking.
