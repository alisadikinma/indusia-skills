# Reference 06 — Creative Vetos (Auto-Reject List)

## Tujuan File Ini

**Hard auto-veto** untuk setiap output content (video script, carousel, post, deck slide). Kalau salah satu element ini muncul, **tolak production** — kembalikan ke writer untuk revisi sebelum lanjut.

Veto ini **non-negotiable**. Bukan "preference" — itu **brand voice violation**.

### Indonesian Corporate Cringe

❌ Banned phrases:

- "Mewujudkan impian Anda"
- "Raih kesuksesan bersama kami"
- "Pelanggan adalah raja"
- "Kerja keras pasti membuahkan hasil"
- "Era digitalisasi"
- "Sentuhan teknologi"
- "Hadir untuk Anda"
- "Berkomitmen memberikan yang terbaik"
- "Solusi untuk semua kebutuhan Anda"
- "Karena Anda layak mendapatkan yang lebih"

### Translation Leak (Indikator Translate dari English Generic)

❌ Banned (suspicious phrasing yang biasanya dari template English):

- "Memberdayakan bisnis Anda" (= empower your business)
- "Membuka potensi penuh" (= unlock full potential)
- "Berkembang ke level berikutnya" (= grow to next level)
- "Mengubah cara Anda berbisnis" (= change the way you do business)
- "Pengalaman yang mulus" (= seamless experience)
- "Solusi all-in-one" (kalau gak ada konteks specific)

### Hedging Loyo

❌ Banned:

- "Mungkin bisa membantu"
- "Sedikit lebih baik"
- "Salah satu pilihan untuk..."
- "Cukup baik"
- "Boleh dicoba"

PT INDUSIA bicara dengan **opinionated confidence**, BUKAN hedging.

### Klise Hook

❌ Banned (overused, instantly recognizable as template):

- "Bayangkan jika..." (kalau dipakai sebagai opener generic)
- "Apakah Anda lelah dengan..." (saat tanpa pain spesifik)
- "Sudah waktunya untuk..." (urgensi palsu)
- "Tahukah Anda bahwa..." (lecture-y)
- "Di era yang serba cepat ini..." (template otomatis)
- "Dalam dunia yang semakin kompetitif..." (template)

## Category B — Banned Visuals (Auto-Veto)

### Stock Footage Cliche

❌ Banned (instantly identifiable as cheap stock):

- Orang business-people shaking hands above table dengan latar putih
- 2-3 orang look at laptop screen smiling exaggerated
- Computer screen with stock-graph upward arrow generic
- Spinning globe blue-white world-map metaphor
- Hand-touching tablet glowing screen sci-fi
- Diverse-team-pose pamflet style
- Walking-in-corridor look-at-camera HR-video aesthetic
- Time-lapse city skyline cliche urban
- Hand-drawing-graph-on-glass futuristic
- Drone-fly-over-office-building (kecuali konteks spesifik)

### Motion Graphics Cliche

❌ Banned:

- 3D logo spinning at intro (5+ seconds dedicated)
- "Loading bar fills 0% to 100%" sebagai metafor progress
- Particle effects fancy untuk decorative purpose
- Glitch effect untuk "edgy tech" aesthetic
- Hexagon-grid honeycomb futuristic background
- Lens flare excessive (1 boleh, 5 banned)
- Lower-third name card dengan blur background animated busy
- Word-by-word text animation untuk semua line of VO (bisa untuk hook moments only)

### Color Grade Banned

❌ Banned:

- Glossy luxury (high saturation deep magenta) — disconnect dari working-class trucking audience
- Hyper-orange-teal Hollywood action LUT
- Anime/Pixar saturation pump
- Full B&W stylized (kecuali concept spesifik)
- Vintage VHS/8mm filter (gimmick)

### Typography Cringe

❌ Banned fonts:

- Comic Sans (obvious)
- Papyrus (obvious)
- Decorative scripts untuk B2B (Pacifico, Lobster, Dancing Script)
- Mixed 3+ fonts in 1 video — chaotic
- Bouncing letter-by-letter animation
- Word-art neon (OK kalau pure decorative moment, banned untuk body)

## Category C — Banned Audio (Auto-Veto)

### Music Cliche

❌ Banned:

- "Corporate uplifting bombastis" (orchestral swell every 5 sec) — feels manipulative + generic
- Trap / hip-hop untuk audience 35-55 owner trucking — disconnect demographic
- EDM drop — gimmick territory
- "Inspirational piano corporate" template (Epidemic Sound default that's been used 1000x)
- Aggressive sub-bass action movie trailer beat
- Orchestral swell pas hook (kayak Pixar emotional moment) — too saccharine

### VO Cliche

❌ Banned:

- "Corporate AI-generated voice" — Synthesia voice yang flat artificial
- "Radio DJ energetic" — too much pep, feels insincere
- "Movie trailer deep voice" — gimmick, mismatch B2B
- Foreign accent sok-sokan (US accent untuk audience Indonesia) — disconnect
- Whisper-style ASMR — wrong context
- Robot-voice synthetic — sounds cheap

### SFX Cliche

❌ Banned:

- "Whoosh" tiap transition — gimmick
- "Cha-ching" cash register saat sebut savings
- "Ding!" notification spam
- Heart-beat saat pain agitate (kalau dipakai >1 detik)

## Category D — Banned Claims (Substantive Veto)

### Over-Promise Banned

❌ Banned claims (kecuali kami punya bukti hard):

- "Eliminate 100% kencing solar" — over-promise, mitigation tetap perlu
- "Increase revenue 50%" tanpa case study quantified
- "Pay back in 6 months" tanpa real customer data
- "Zero downtime guaranteed" — software always has edge cases
- "AI predicts perfectly" — ML imperfect by nature

✅ OK kalau ada disclaimer atau case-study-specific:

- "Pada IRN target reduction kencing solar 70-80%" (kasih range, source pilot)
- "Sopir Anda lihat komisi real-time, bukan akhir bulan" — concrete capability claim

### Investment / ROI Numbers (Untuk Public Promo)

❌ Banned di **public promo content** (TVC video, carousel public, LinkedIn organic):

- "Investasi Rp X juta" — itu sales pitch, BUKAN promo
- "ROI 30% dalam 12 bulan"
- "Payback period 18 bulan"
- "Save Rp Y juta per tahun" sebagai HEADLINE (boleh sebagai outcome cerita kalau frame ringkas, tapi BUKAN headline value-prop)

✅ OK di **pitch deck / sales meeting / email proposal lampiran**:

- Detail CapEx breakdown
- ROI projection
- Pricing tier
- Cost-benefit comparison

### Tech Buzzword Tanpa Concrete

❌ Banned:

- "Powered by blockchain" (kecuali fitur blockchain real, bukan marketing)
- "Quantum-ready" (irrelevant)
- "Web3-enabled" (irrelevant untuk logistics)
- "Metaverse-integrated" (cringe)
- "AGI-level AI" (over-claim)
- "Self-aware system" (over-claim)

## Category E — Banned Format Mismatches

### Audience Mismatch

❌ Banned:

- 60-second TikTok-style fast-cut untuk Pak Indra archetype (50+ owner) — overwhelming
- Long-form 10-min documentary untuk Cici archetype (Gen-Y, attention span shorter) — kalau bisa 90s lebih baik
- Pitch deck slide dengan tone "tabloid" — wrong for B2B context
- LinkedIn post dengan emoji-spam 30+ emoji — looks like personal account

### CTA Mismatch

❌ Banned:

- "Klik link di bio" untuk **B2B proposal video** — too social-style
- "DM us!!!" dengan multiple exclamation — cringe
- "Limited time offer" (urgency palsu) — distrustful
- "Don't miss out" — cliche
- "Subscribe + like" untuk B2B — wrong platform

### Format Mismatch

❌ Banned:

- 4 menit video tanpa subtitle/caption — Pak Indra mute play di office
- Pitch deck slide >150 kata per slide — gak readable
- Carousel slide tanpa visual hierarchy — semua text size sama
- Email body dengan banyak emoji — wrong tone B2B

## Auto-Veto Checklist (Sebelum Approve Production)

Run setiap content draft thru ini. Kalau ada ❌ = stop, return for revision.

### Content Audit

- [ ] No banned vocabulary (Section A)?
- [ ] No translation-leak phrasing?
- [ ] No corporate cringe Indonesian?
- [ ] No hedging language?
- [ ] No klise hook opener?

### Visual Audit

- [ ] No stock footage cliche?
- [ ] No motion graphics overdrive?
- [ ] Color grade fit doctrine (industrial-doc desaturated default)?
- [ ] Typography max 2 fonts, no Comic Sans/Papyrus?
- [ ] No 3D logo spinning intro >2 sec?

### Audio Audit

- [ ] No corporate-uplifting bombastis music?
- [ ] No "inspirational piano" template?
- [ ] VO match archetype (lihat 02 archetype routing)?
- [ ] No SFX overdrive (whoosh tiap transition, etc)?

### Claims Audit

- [ ] No over-promise (100%, zero, perfect)?
- [ ] Investment numbers ABSENT (kalau public promo)?
- [ ] Tech buzzword grounded di concrete feature?

### Format Audit

- [ ] Length match format intended (3-4 min proposal video, 60s social, etc)?
- [ ] CTA appropriate untuk format + audience?
- [ ] Subtitles ready untuk mute play?

### Strategic Audit

- [ ] PT INDUSIA = speaker, IRN = subject jelas?
- [ ] Specific IRN data referenced ("23 truk Anda" not "armada Anda")?
- [ ] 1 mechanism per video (atau cluster fokus, gak overload)?
- [ ] Pain agitate sebelum solusi (sopir 5L solar before sensor reveal)?
- [ ] CTA tidak terlalu hard-sell di public-facing?

## Veto Output Format (Saat Saya Reject Draft)

```
🚫 VETO — Cannot Produce

VIOLATIONS FOUND:
1. [Category]: [Specific issue]
   Location: [line/timestamp/slide]
   Why veto: [reasoning rooted in doctrine file]
   Alternative: [concrete fix suggestion]

2. ...

ACTIONS:
- Return to creator with this feedback
- Re-validate after revision
- Loop max 2× then escalate to user
```

Example:

```
🚫 VETO — Script Draft Cannot Produce

VIOLATIONS:

1. Banned vocab: "Solusi terbaik untuk transformasi digital Anda" 
   (Hook, line 1)
   Why: 4 banned phrases stacked. Sounds like Big-4 IT 
        consultant, not PT INDUSIA's lapangan-savvy voice.
   Alternative: "Sopir Anda nyolong solar. Kami punya jawaban."

2. Stock footage cliche: storyboard mention "diverse team 
   shaking hands" (Beat 4)
   Why: instantly identifiable as cheap stock, kills credibility.
   Alternative: actual lapangan B-roll — Pak Indra di kantor, 
   Cici di HP, sopir di kabin.

3. Investment number in hook: "Investasi sistem kami Rp 100jt"
   Why: hard rule — investment numbers banned di public promo. 
   Audience close di hook.
   Alternative: lead with pain, save investment for pitch deck 
   pricing slide later.

ACTIONS:
- Return to writer dengan 3 fix di atas.
- Re-validate via /linkedin-validate atau /video-validate 
  setelah revision.
```
