# Reference 02 — Archetype Routing & Hook Selection

## Project Variable Lock

Archetype-archetype di bawah (Pak Indra, Bu Rinda, Cici, Pak Hamdardi, Pak Eko, Pak Bambang) adalah **persona pattern untuk B2B logistik Indonesia** — applicable ke any project (bukan IRN-only).

Saat invoke skill untuk project lain:
- **Substitute nama** kalau client tahu real persona name (e.g., "Pak Budi" untuk owner Y)
- **Substitute data spesifik** (fleet size, lokasi, customer mix)
- **Tambah/kurangi archetype** sesuai client decision team

Pattern voice + hook recommendation tetap valid untuk tiap archetype lintas-project.

---

## Archetype-First Creative Decision Framework

Setiap creative decision dimulai dengan **archetype**. Bukan dengan "format" atau "platform". Tanpa archetype yang jelas → semua keputusan jadi gut-feel.

```
ARCHETYPE → AWARENESS LEVEL → HOOK TYPE → TONE → FORMAT
```

## 6 Archetype IRN (dari pakar-logistik-batam/06)

### Archetype 1 — "Pak Indra" (Owner Trucking 20-50 Unit)

**Demographic:**
- 35-55 tahun, pria, mantan sopir / mantan admin yang naik kelas
- Office Bengkong / Batu Ampar, modal Rp 5-15M
- WA + Excel dominant, kadang Whatsapp web
- Net worth modest (bisnis = aset utama)

**Pain Emosional Utama:** *Kehilangan kontrol* — sopir nyolong, customer molor, admin lalai, semua di luar visibility

**Awareness Level:** Problem-Aware (sudah sadar pain, belum tahu solusi)

**Hook Type yang Resonate:**
| Type | Score | Reasoning |
|---|---|---|
| **Data Hook (Loss Aversion)** ⭐⭐⭐ | 9/10 | Angka pain konkret. Pak Indra sudah pegang Excel kasbon — angka bicara |
| **Story Hook (Specific Driver Story)** ⭐⭐⭐ | 9/10 | "Sopir saya Pak Y, 5 tahun kerja, ternyata..." resonate |
| **Pattern Interrupt** ⭐⭐ | 7/10 | Bisa, tapi jangan terlalu artsy |
| **Question Hook** ⭐⭐ | 7/10 | "Tahu gak berapa truk Anda nyolong?" — bisa |
| **Aspirational Hook** ⭐ | 4/10 | "Wujudkan masa depan logistik" — too corporate |

**Tone Recommended:**
- Lugas-urgent
- Empathetic (sebut nama archetype eksplisit "Pak Indra")
- Dry humor (sopir-pinter-vs-system metaphor)
- BUKAN: corporate-aspirational, BUKAN: motivasional

**Visual Style:** Industrial-doc desaturated, sopir POV B-roll, dashboard demo screen-record

**Music Style:** Tense-build (60-80 BPM) → strategic silence at peak

**VO Voice:** Pria 40-50, gritty timbre, sedikit aksen Sumatera/Melayu Batam

**Sample VO Hook (3 variasi siap pakai):**
1. *"Anda 30 truk. 30% sopir Anda nyolong solar. 5 liter per trip. Hitung sendiri hari ini."*
2. *"Pak Indra punya sistem yang bagus — di Excel. Tapi sopirnya nyolong solar 6 bulan, baru ketahuan."*
3. *"Sopir Pak Indra antar kontainer Hamin → Tg Uncang. Pulang... mampir SPBU. Bukan untuk isi solar — untuk JUAL solar."*

**CTA Style:** Soft. *"Selengkapnya di [link bio / DM kami]"* — Pak Indra perlu chat dulu sebelum siap evaluate solusi.

**Format Recommended:**
- ⭐ TikTok 60s vertical (testimonial mock atau stop-motion B-roll)
- LinkedIn carousel 1080×1350 (untuk yang scroll LinkedIn)
- IG Reels 30s (versi shorter dari TikTok)

### Archetype 2 — "Bu Rinda" (Akuntan / Owner Wife)

**Demographic:**
- 30-50 tahun, perempuan
- Pegang buku kas, kasbon driver, faktur pajak
- HP Android, kerja remote dari rumah
- Detail-oriented, angka-driven, tapi non-technical

**Pain Emosional Utama:** Burnout dari manual data + closing buku berantakan

**Awareness Level:** Problem-Aware → Solution-Aware

**Hook Type yang Resonate:**
| Type | Score | Reasoning |
|---|---|---|
| **Story Hook (Day-in-Life)** ⭐⭐⭐ | 10/10 | "Ibu Rinda, hari Jumat malam masih buka Excel..." instant resonance |
| **Data Hook (Time Pain)** ⭐⭐⭐ | 9/10 | "10 hari nutup buku" — concrete time pain |
| **Question Hook** ⭐⭐ | 7/10 | "Anda close buku berapa hari?" |
| **Pattern Interrupt** ⭐⭐ | 6/10 | Less effective for this archetype |
| **Aspirational** ⭐⭐ | 6/10 | "Bisa nutup buku 1 hari" — boleh tapi hanya at peak/won-day |

**Tone Recommended:**
- Hangat, empathetic, validating ("kerja Ibu lelah, kami tahu")
- BUKAN: kasar, BUKAN: corporate

**Visual Style:** Warm-human (warm tint 3200K), close-up Bu Rinda di laptop malam, family photo di background subtle

**Music Style:** Warm-acoustic (70-90 BPM), solo guitar atau light piano

**VO Voice:** Perempuan 30-40, tenang, professional, hangat — BUKAN energetic radio voice

**Sample VO Hook:**
1. *"Jumat malam, jam 11. Bu Rinda masih buka Excel. Faktur pajak November belum tutup."*
2. *"Suami punya 30 truk. Bu Rinda yang harus reconcile 247 surat jalan tiap akhir bulan. Sendirian."*
3. *"10 hari kerja keras Bu Rinda. Untuk apa? 90% data entry yang harusnya otomatis."*

**CTA Style:** Soft + value-promising. *"Bayangkan punya Sabtu lagi. Kontak kami di [link]."*

**Format Recommended:**
- ⭐ Facebook video 90s (Bu Rinda demographic FB > TikTok)
- Instagram Reels 60s
- LinkedIn post 1100 chars dengan story

### Archetype 3 — "Cici" (Admin Dispatcher)

**Demographic:**
- 25-40 tahun, perempuan (sometimes pria)
- 6-7 hari kerja, WA group dengan 30+ sopir
- HP standby pukul 5 pagi (kapal sandar early)
- Tech-comfortable (gen-Y/millennial)

**Pain Emosional Utama:** Overwhelm dari WA chaos + jadi "sistem hidup" — manusia jadi data router

**Awareness Level:** Problem-Aware (sudah merasakan, tapi belum tahu cara fix)

**Hook Type:**
| Type | Score |
|---|---|
| **Story Hook (Overwhelm Day)** ⭐⭐⭐ | 10/10 |
| **Pattern Interrupt** ⭐⭐⭐ | 9/10 (Gen-Y respond to fast-paced) |
| **Data Hook (Time)** ⭐⭐⭐ | 8/10 |
| **Question Hook** ⭐⭐ | 7/10 |

**Tone:** Energetic, sympathetic, slightly humorous (relatable burnout)

**Visual:** Cici close-up, multiple phones ringing, WhatsApp chat fast scroll overlay, eyes tired

**Music:** Driving documentary 90-110 BPM atau strategic silence + WhatsApp ping SFX

**VO Voice:** Perempuan 25-35, clear, energetic, **relatable bukan polished**

**Sample VO Hook:**
1. *"5 pagi. HP Cici sudah 50 chat. Belum mandi. Belum kopi."*
2. *"Cici dispatcher 30 sopir. Foto delivery sopir? Hilang di WA, di antara meme dan grup arisan."*
3. *"Anda pikir admin Anda kerja 8 jam? Coba lihat HP-nya jam 9 malam."*

**CTA:** Direct, action-oriented. *"Kasih HP Cici Anda istirahat. Demo: [link]"*

**Format Recommended:**
- ⭐ Instagram Reels 30-45s (Cici demographic)
- TikTok 60s
- IG carousel before/after WhatsApp chaos vs dashboard clean

### Archetype 4 — "Pak Hamdardi" (Sopir Senior)

**Demographic:**
- 30-50 tahun, pria, sopir 5+ tahun
- HP Android Rp 1-3jt
- Skeptis terhadap aplikasi (sebelumnya disuruh pakai SITC app, ribet)
- Konservatif, repeat-customer mindset

**Pain Emosional Utama:** Capek di-tagih admin "fotonya mana", komisi kadang gak jelas, gak dihargai sebagai professional

**Awareness Level:** Problem-Aware tapi NOT solution-seeking — solusi datang via owner's mandate

**Hook Type:**
| Type | Score |
|---|---|
| **UGC-Style Direct Address** ⭐⭐⭐ | 10/10 — sopir asli bicara |
| **Story Hook (Sopir POV)** ⭐⭐⭐ | 9/10 |
| **Question Hook** ⭐⭐ | 6/10 |
| **Data Hook** ⭐ | 4/10 — sopir gak peduli stat fleet, peduli komisi |
| **Pattern Interrupt fancy** ⭐ | 3/10 — too modern, putting off |

**Tone:** Lugas, langsung dari hati, bahasa lapangan ("ini juta, itu komisi")

**Visual:** UGC raw — sopir di kabin truk, smartphone POV, NO produksi mahal

**Music:** Minimalist atau no music — let the words land

**VO Voice:** Sopir asli (bukan VO actor), aksen daerah natural, **otentik > polished**

**Sample VO Hook (UGC style):**
1. Sopir asli ngomong: *"Saya udah 5 tahun nyetir trailer. Dulu, foto pengantaran kirim WA — sering hilang. Komisi telat. Sekarang tap aplikasi, beres."*
2. *"Pas saya dapat aplikasi baru, mikir 'ah pasti ribet kayak yang lalu'. Ternyata cuma 2 tombol."*
3. *"Komisi saya bulan November jelas, gak bingung. Bonus efisiensi BBM tambah 200rb. Lumayan."*

**CTA:** Untuk owner audience yang nonton: *"Kasih sopir Anda alat yang dia mau pakai. Bukan yang Anda pikir dia butuh."*

**Format Recommended:**
- ⭐ Testimonial UGC TikTok/Reels 60s
- YouTube Shorts 60s
- WhatsApp Status video 30s (untuk sopir share ke sopir lain)

**Special Note:** Pak Hamdardi BUKAN target ad — dia subject of testimonial. Audience ad untuk **owner yang melihat sopir-nya bicara**.

### Archetype 5 — "Pak Eko" (Project Manager Shipyard)

**Demographic:**
- 35-50 tahun, engineering background
- Tanggung jawab project timeline + safety + budget shipyard
- Email + project management software user
- Decision authority untuk vendor crane

**Pain Emosional Utama:** Vendor crane delay / non-compliant → project delay → bonus terancam

**Awareness Level:** Solution-Aware (sudah pernah kena vendor non-compliant)

**Hook Type:**
| Type | Score |
|---|---|
| **Data Hook (Compliance Pain)** ⭐⭐⭐ | 10/10 |
| **Story Hook (Audit Nightmare)** ⭐⭐⭐ | 9/10 |
| **Question Hook** ⭐⭐ | 7/10 |
| **Pattern Interrupt** ⭐⭐ | 6/10 |

**Tone:** Authoritative, technical-confident, slight English-Indonesian mix

**Visual:** Crisp corporate-restrained, shipyard B-roll, certificate close-up, dashboard compliance digital

**Music:** Documentary mid-tempo 90-110 BPM, restrained

**VO Voice:** Pria 40-55, authoritative, technical, slight English mix

**Sample VO Hook:**
1. *"Crane vendor Anda tiba di shipyard Senin pagi. SIO operator-nya? Habis 4 bulan lalu. Project Anda? Tertunda 2 hari."*
2. *"HSE audit Anda 30 hari lagi. Vendor crane Anda punya digital trail? Atau berkas masih di dus di gudang?"*
3. *"PM yang pintar pilih vendor crane bukan dari harga termurah. Dari compliance trail tercatat."*

**CTA:** Direct + value-promising. *"Lihat vendor compliance live di [link]"*

**Format Recommended:**
- ⭐ LinkedIn 90s video
- B2B email mp4 attachment
- Pitch deck embed video
- YouTube 3-min explainer

### Archetype 6 — "Pak Bambang" (EPC O&G Operations Lead)

**Demographic:**
- 40-55 tahun, technical leadership
- Audit-heavy environment (HSE, environmental, safety)
- Project value Rp 50M+, cancel cost super tinggi
- Risk-averse, paperwork-heavy

**Pain Emosional Utama:** Safety incident di project = HSE escalation, project diaudit, blacklist

**Awareness Level:** Solution-Aware (very experienced — knows what they need)

**Hook Type:** Same as Pak Eko (engineering archetype) tapi **higher technical depth**, lower humor

**Sample VO Hook:**
1. *"Cargo Anda ke fasilitas oil & gas Kabil. Vendor trucking Anda? Audit trail digital, atau verbal commitment?"*
2. *"HSE audit menemukan crane operator tanpa SIO valid. Project audit, contract review, possible blacklist. 1 cert lewat = 3 bulan recovery."*

**Format Recommended:**
- LinkedIn long-form video 3 min
- B2B email
- Pitch deck untuk procurement meeting

## Addendum (Day R3, 2026-05-11) — WS1-REDO Video Psychology + Owner Visibility Gap Hook Patterns

> **Anchor — All 7 Owner Visibility Gaps as hook material:** Setiap hook WAJIB anchor di "tahu" vs "tidak tahu" tension. Format: *"Anda tahu [specific data] tadi pagi?"* — direct address owner, specific data, owner-pov. JANGAN industry-pain "ada masalah X" framing.

### Quantified Hook Timing (WS1-REDO findings)

| Window | Rule | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **0-3 seconds** | Visual + audio + curiosity-gap delivered BEFORE 3 sec mark | WS1 REDO: 3-second rule TikTok/IG algorithmic |
| **0-30 seconds** | Algorithmic cliff — if engagement <X%, video buried | WS1: 30-sec retention as primary algorithm signal |
| **3-7 seconds** | Foreshadow setup — promise of payoff later | WS1: dopamine on anticipation, not on reward |
| **70-85% runtime** | Peak placement | WS6 narrative framework (5-beat) |
| **CTA last 10-15%** | Soft for B2B (consult / DM / WA), hard for B2C | WS1 + WS6 |

### Indonesian Low-Arousal Register (WS1-REDO)

Indonesian audience attention pattern ≠ Western high-arousal hook. Default register:
- **Calm + matter-of-fact** delivery beats shouty hook
- **Data-specific** beats vague hyperbole ("23 truk Anda" beats "fleet besar")
- **Direct address owner** ("Bapak / Anda") beats third-person ("para pengusaha")
- **Quiet conviction** beats high-energy salesman
- **Local accent / lokal idiom** preserves authenticity (NOT corporate-sterile Bahasa baku)

### Owner Visibility Gap Curiosity-Gap Hook Library (canonical for IRN + reusable)

Pattern: *"Anda tahu [specific gap data] tadi [time anchor]?"* — opens curiosity gap (Loewenstein) at owner-pov.

| Gap # | Hook Pattern Example |
|---|---|
| 1 (kondisi lapangan) | *"Pak, Anda tahu sekarang jam 14:30, truk Anda yang B-1234 lagi di mana? Sopir Rohim mungkin... di customer? Antri di gate? Atau lagi makan siang ke-3 kalinya hari ini?"* |
| 2 (ETA driver) | *"Anda quote customer 'sampai jam 4'. Sopir Anda berangkat jam 11. Anda tahu — APA YANG SEHARUSNYA jadi ETA-nya? Atau Anda nebak-nebak aja seperti customer Anda?"* |
| 3 (solar) | *"50 liter masuk SPBU jam 9:15. Tank Anda cuma naik 35 liter. Anda tahu 15 liter sisanya ke mana? Atau Anda gak tau ada yang missing?"* |
| 4 (attendance) | *"Karyawan Anda kemarin masuk jam berapa? Pulang jam berapa? Bukan menurut absensi paper. Menurut RFID gate. Anda tahu bedanya?"* |
| 5 (container jalan) | *"Container EISU456789 yang Anda kontrak ke customer Batamindo — sekarang dia di Trans Batam, di pelabuhan, atau di rumah sopir? Anda tahu jawabannya?"* |
| 6 (chassis) | *"90 chassis Anda. 10 menit yang lalu, di mana mereka semua? Pak Indra... Anda tahu, atau Anda 'kira-kira' aja?"* |
| 7 (container gudang) | *"Gudang Anda 200 slot. Container Anda 47. Coba tunjuk peta — slot mana yang B5C3, yang harus keluar besok pagi? Anda tahu... atau cari dulu 20 menit?"* |

**Apply rule:** Setiap video promo / carousel / pitch deck hook WAJIB pakai 1 dari 7 pattern di atas (atau adapt). Triangulate ke 3-pillar di body video (AI/IoT/Mobile yang solve specific gap).

### Foreshadow Pattern (WS1 + WS6 input)

Dopamine triggers pada **anticipation** of resolution, not on resolution itself. Body of video harus drip-feed Asisten/dashboard reveal:
- Sec 5-10: pertanyaan/pain hook (owner gap)
- Sec 10-25: hint solusi ada ("Pak Indra punya cara baru lihat ini...")
- Sec 25-40: Asisten on-screen, sample interaction (WhatsApp screen rendering)
- Sec 40-60: payoff moment — Pak Indra ngomong "Saya bisa lihat semua dari HP saya"

### Narrative Transportation (WS1)

Story-driven video bypasses ELM (Elaboration Likelihood Model) — viewer suspend disbelief, no counter-arguing. **Default for Pak Indra archetype owner promo = story-driven (owner di kantor / kopitiam, HP getar, Asisten kasih tahu)**, NOT data-driven (chart bar showing fleet utilization). Data-driven cadangan untuk pitch deck B2B procurement audience.

### Awareness-Level Pillar Routing (WS9 differentiator)

Tionghoa Batam owner = **practical-skeptic** awareness level (between "problem-aware" dan "solution-aware"). Hook strategy:
- DO: Quantify cost of NOT knowing ("Rp 5-15jt/bulan leak solar")
- DO: Show comparable competitor implementing (Eddi has TMS, IRN need owner-pov differentiation)
- DON'T: Abstract "digital transformation" (gets dismissed)
- DON'T: Spiritual/aspirational framing (Tionghoa register practical, not transcendent — see LOCK-4)
