# Reference 01 — Brand Voice Doctrine

## Single Source of Truth

File ini **wajib di-load** untuk setiap creative decision (video, carousel, pitch deck, LinkedIn, ad copy, web copy). Brand voice doctrine **tidak berbeda per format** — yang berubah hanya panjang dan medium.

## Project Variable Lock (LOCK SEBELUM PRODUCE)

Tiap project, lock variabel berikut. **Examples below use PT INDUSIA × IRN sebagai illustration. Substitute untuk project Anda.**

```
SPEAKER (Brand Voice Owner):  {{agency_name}}
                              [default illustrative: PT INDUSIA Kecerdasan Digital]

CUSTOMER (Subject / Audience): {{client_name}}
                              [default illustrative: PT IRN]

CLIENT INDUSTRY:              {{client_industry}}
                              [default illustrative: container trucking + crane rental Batam]

VIDEO PURPOSE:                {{video_purpose}}
                              [default: B2B proposal video / sales pitch]

DECISION MAKERS AT CLIENT:    {{client_decision_makers}}
                              [archetype-based: owner / admin / akuntan / PM / etc]
```

**Voice doctrine berikut applicable ke ANY agency/client kombinasi**, asal:
- Speaker = AI/digital/tech agency Indonesia (B2B)
- Client = perusahaan logistik / B2B industrial / fleet operator Indonesia
- Audience = decision makers di client (owner-archetype, admin-archetype, dll)

Kalau ada konflik antara doctrine ini dan permintaan user, **flag ke user** sebelum produce.

## 1. Brand Voice Personality — PT INDUSIA (5-Trait Model)

| Trait | PT INDUSIA Voice | Generic IT Agency |
|---|---|---|
| **Tone** | Tech-credible + lapangan-savvy | Polished corporate buzzword |
| **Authority** | Earned (kami pernah di kabin truk) + technical | Borrowed (slick deck only) |
| **Empathy** | Specific (sebut Pak Indra/Cici by archetype) | Generic ("para pengusaha") |
| **Optimism** | Earned (after pain agitate via case study) | Default-bright |
| **Humor** | Dry, observational, sometimes self-deprecating about tech industry | Bombastis atau absent |
| **Tech Cred** | Concrete (sebut "AI" + "digital" hanya saat related to specific feature) | Buzzword salad ("AI-powered ecosystem") |

**One-line summary:** "Agency AI Indonesia yang ngerti kabin truk, bukan cuma slide deck. Kami solve pain Pak Indra — dan kami bicara bahasanya."

**One-line POSITIONING (vs kompetitor):**
- ❌ Big-4 IT consultant: "Transformasi digital yang holistik" — tidak relate
- ❌ Generic agency: "Solusi software berbasis AI" — tidak credible
- ✅ **PT INDUSIA: "AI yang solve sopir nyolong solar, bukan AI yang main slide deck"**

## 2. Vocabulary — Do Use

### Local Industry Terms (Wajib pakai saat audience tahu)
- Kencing solar, cas, supir, sopir, kasbon, surat jalan
- SP2, Hamin 2/3, Batu Ampar, Tanjung Uncang, Sekupang, Kabil
- Bottom Centre (Batam Centre), Batamindo, Tg Pinang
- Hari kering (no rain, no problem), jam padat, banjir kontainer

### Bilingual Technical Terms (gunakan English saat sudah English domain)
- TMS, ePoD, GPS, telematics, demurrage, detention
- Working capital, DSO (Days Sales Outstanding)
- ROI, P&L, margin, capex, opex
- SILO, SIO, K3 (Permenaker terminology — leave as is)

### Numerical Specificity — Format-Spesifik!

**Video promo public (TOP-OF-FUNNEL):**
- ✅ Angka **PAIN** ("5 liter per trip", "75 hari payment terms", "12 sopir") — itu resonance trigger
- ❌ Angka **INVESTASI/ROI** ("CapEx Rp X jt", "ROI 30%", "Payback 18 bulan") — itu sales pitch material, BUKAN promo
- ❌ Angka harga produk
- ✅ Angka **OUTCOME** secara metaphor ("berhenti kehilangan jutaan", "stop bocor") — emotional, bukan financial calc

**Pitch deck / sales meeting / case study (BOTTOM-OF-FUNNEL):**
- ✅ Full numerical detail OK (CapEx, ROI, payback, monthly cost)
- ✅ Tabel cost-benefit
- ✅ Competitive pricing comparison

**Article / blog post:**
- ✅ Pain numbers prominent
- ⚠️ Investment numbers OK kalau article-nya specifically about "calculating ROI"
- ❌ Hindari di intro/hook — turunkan engagement

**LinkedIn post:**
- ✅ Pain numbers
- ⚠️ Investment numbers HANYA kalau case study format
- ❌ Tidak boleh di hook awal

**Mengapa rule ini penting:** customer Pak Indra di awareness "problem-aware" tapi belum tahu solusi. Kalau hook-nya "Investasi sistem kami Rp 100 juta", dia close. Kalau hook-nya "Sopir Anda nyolong 5 liter/trip", dia stay → baru di sales call dia siap dengar harga.

## 3. Vocabulary — DON'T Use (Auto-Veto List)

### Corporate Jargon Banned
❌ "Sinergi", "ekosistem yang terintegrasi", "transformasi digital"  
❌ "Optimalisasi rantai pasok", "efisiensi operasional menyeluruh"  
❌ "Solusi terbaik untuk bisnis Anda", "mitra terpercaya"  
❌ "Inovatif", "revolusioner", "world-class", "best-in-class"  
❌ "AI-powered" (tanpa konkret feature mention)  
❌ "Seamless", "frictionless", "robust", "cutting-edge"  

### Indonesian Klise Banned
❌ "Kekinian", "wujudkan impian", "raih kesuksesan"  
❌ "Kebahagiaan", "kebanggaan", "kepuasan" (terlalu generic emotional)  
❌ "Pelanggan adalah raja", "kerja keras pasti membuahkan hasil"  

### Translation Leak (Indikator Translate dari English Generic)
❌ "Memberdayakan bisnis Anda" (empower your business)  
❌ "Membuka potensi penuh" (unlock full potential)  
❌ "Berkembang ke level berikutnya" (grow to next level)  

### Emotional-Manipulation Cliche
❌ "Bayangkan jika..." (saat dipakai sebagai opener generic)  
❌ "Apakah Anda lelah..." (saat tanpa pain spesifik)  
❌ "Sudah waktunya untuk..." (urgensi palsu)

## 4. Sentence Structure Rules

### DO
- **Pendek di pain.** "Sopir Anda nyolong 5 liter. Hari ini." (2 kalimat, dampak tinggi)
- **Spesifik di angka.** "Anda 50 truk. 30% sopir. Rp 124jt setahun."
- **Simple Present di voiceover.** "Ini terjadi tiap minggu." > "Hal ini akan terjadi setiap minggunya."
- **Direct address.** "Pak Indra," > "Para pengusaha trucking,"
- **Comma-pause untuk efek.** "Tarif Anda... potong BBM... potong komisi... sisa Anda."

### DON'T
- ❌ Kalimat majemuk dengan 3+ klausa (corporate-itis)
- ❌ Pasif voice ("dilakukan optimasi") — pakai aktif
- ❌ Buzzword stacking ("solusi end-to-end yang AI-powered untuk transformasi digital")
- ❌ Hedging ("mungkin bisa membantu sedikit") — jangan pakai voice yang loyo
- ❌ Cap lock untuk emosi (cringe in copy)

## 5. Color Grade Doctrine

### IRN Visual Language

| Mode | Color Profile | Use Case |
|---|---|---|
| **Industrial Documentary** ⭐ default | Desaturated, slight cool tint, blacks crushed, highlights muted | Pain story, B-roll lapangan, sopir testimony |
| **Warm Human** | Warm tint (3200K), skin tones natural, slight grain | Cici/Bu Rinda close-up, family-emergency story |
| **Crisp Corporate** (use sparingly) | Neutral white balance, clean blacks, controlled saturation | Dashboard demo, data visualization |
| **Cinematic Hope** (peak/CTA only) | Slight orange-teal but RESTRAINED, sun-flare allowed | Won Day reveal, transformation moment |

### Banned Color Profiles
❌ **Glossy luxury** (high saturation, deep magenta) — disconnect dari working-class trucking audience  
❌ **Hyper-orange-teal Hollywood** (full action movie LUT) — too aggressive, gimmick territory  
❌ **Anime/Pixar saturation** — wrong demographic  
❌ **Full B&W stylized** (kecuali specific creative concept dengan reasoning) — feels arty without justification  

### Reference LUTs (jika pakai DaVinci/Premiere)
- "Documentary Desaturated" — base
- "ARRI Alexa Natural" — for human close-ups
- AVOID: "Hollywood Action", "Glossy Lifestyle"

## 6. Music Doctrine

### Mood Palette (BPM range)

| Mood | BPM | Instruments | When |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Strategic Silence** | 0 | None | Pattern interrupt 1.7s, peak moment buildup |
| **Industrial Tension** | 60-80 | Sub-bass drone, single percussive hit, low piano | Pain agitate beat |
| **Warm Acoustic** | 70-90 | Solo guitar, light percussion, natural sound | Empathy / human story |
| **Driving Documentary** | 90-110 | Mid-tempo drums, ambient pads, sparse melody | Guide+Plan beat |
| **Earned Uplift** | 100-120 | Piano + strings building, percussion accent | Peak / Won Day |
| **VO-Only** | n/a | No music, ambient SFX only | Testimonial, technical demo |

### Banned Music Categories
❌ **Corporate uplifting bombastis** (orchestral swell every 5 sec) — feels manipulative + generic  
❌ **Trap / hip-hop** untuk audience 35-55 owner trucking — disconnect demographic  
❌ **EDM drop** — gimmick territory  
❌ **"Inspirational piano corporate"** template (Epidemic Sound default that's been used 1000x) — auto-recognize cliche  

### Stock Music Sources Recommended
- **Premium**: Musicbed, Artlist (curated, less cliche)
- **Decent**: Epidemic Sound (filter aggressive — most are cliche)
- **Free**: YouTube Audio Library — only handpicked tracks, usually too generic

### Custom Score Threshold
**Worth budget Rp 5-15jt untuk custom score** kalau:
- Hero campaign 6+ bulan use
- Cross-format scaling (1 score, 10+ video variations)
- Brand anthem video (3-5 menit)

Skip custom score untuk: single-use social video <90 sec.

## 7. Typography (Untuk On-Screen Text)

### Approved Fonts
| Use Case | Font | Reasoning |
|---|---|---|
| **Headline / hook text** | Inter Bold, Helvetica Neue Bold, atau Roboto Bold | Clean, legible at any size |
| **Body / explanation** | Inter Regular | Highly legible mobile |
| **Numerical impact** | Inter Black, Bebas Neue | Visual weight for "Rp 124 juta" big numbers |
| **Quotation / testimonial** | Inter Italic atau handwritten if UGC | Authenticity |

### Banned Fonts
❌ Comic Sans, Papyrus (obvious)  
❌ Decorative scripts untuk B2B (Pacifico, Lobster) — wrong tone  
❌ Mixed 3+ fonts dalam 1 video — chaotic  

### On-Screen Text Rules
- **Hierarchy**: max 2 levels (headline + supporting)
- **Color**: white on dark BG OR brand orange (#F37021 IRN if available, else neutral)
- **Animation**: minimal — fade-in dominant, type-on text accept jika hook beat, NO bouncing
- **Mobile-safe zone**: 10% margin all sides untuk vertical video

## 8. Voiceover Doctrine

### Voice Casting

| Archetype Audience | VO Voice Profile |
|---|---|
| **Pak Indra** (owner) | Pria 40-50, gritty timbre, sedikit aksen Sumatera/Melayu Batam, NOT polished radio-broadcast |
| **Bu Rinda** (akuntan) | Pria atau perempuan 35-45, hangat, calm, professional but not corporate |
| **Cici** (admin) | Perempuan 25-35, clear, energetic, relatable |
| **Pak Hamdardi** (sopir) | UGC raw — sopir asli bicara langsung, bukan VO actor |
| **Pak Eko/Bambang** (B2B PM/EPC) | Pria 40-55, authoritative, technical, slight English-Indonesian mix |

### Pacing
- **Pain agitate**: slow, weighted, pause before reveal numerical
- **Guide+Plan**: medium, confident, build forward momentum
- **Peak**: slow-down at climax word
- **CTA**: clear, direct, no rush

### Recording Standard
- 48kHz, 24-bit minimum
- Treated room (no echo)
- Audacity/Adobe Audition basic NR
- Loudness target: -16 LUFS (TikTok/IG), -14 LUFS (LinkedIn/YouTube)

### Banned VO Styles
❌ "Corporate AI-generated voice" — Synthesia voice yang flat artificial  
❌ "Radio DJ energetic" — too much pep, feels insincere  
❌ "Movie trailer deep voice" — gimmick, mismatch B2B  
❌ Foreign accent sok-sokan (US accent untuk audience Indonesia) — disconnect

## 9. Cross-Format Consistency Matrix

Brand voice **harus identical** across:

| Format | Voice Application |
|---|---|
| **Video script** (VO + on-screen) | Full doctrine apply |
| **Carousel slides** | Same vocab + tone, format-adapted layout |
| **Pitch deck slide** | Same vocab tapi profesional-tweak (sedikit more polished untuk investor) |
| **LinkedIn text post** | Same vocab tapi conversational (1st person OK) |
| **Article long-form** | Expand same voice, deeper |
| **WhatsApp / DM marketing** | Most casual register dari same voice |

**RULE**: kalau orang bisa identify "ini IRN content" tanpa lihat logo, voice doctrine berhasil. Kalau perlu logo untuk identify = doctrine bocor.

## 10. Voice Audit Checklist (Sebelum Publish)

Sebelum approve script/copy/video, cek:

- [ ] Apakah ada banned vocab? (Section 3)
- [ ] Apakah numerikal spesifik? (Section 2 — angka, bukan general)
- [ ] Apakah direct address ke archetype, bukan "para pengusaha"?
- [ ] Apakah pain agitate tidak generik?
- [ ] Apakah color grade match audience (industrial-doc, bukan glossy)?
- [ ] Apakah music BPM dan style match emotional core scene?
- [ ] Apakah VO casting match archetype (bukan corporate radio voice untuk Pak Indra)?
- [ ] Apakah on-screen text hierarchy max 2 levels?
- [ ] Apakah ada gimmick (3D logo, motion graphics berlebih, stock footage cliche)?
- [ ] Apakah doctrine bisa identify ini IRN content tanpa logo?

Kalau >2 ❌ → kembalikan ke creator untuk revisi.

## Addendum (Day R3, 2026-05-11) — Register Calibration + 2-Layer Cultural Model

> **Anchor — Speaker (`{{agency_company}}`, AI+IoT+Mobile Solution agency) vs Subject (client owner archetype).** Brand voice MUST address both: owner layer + workforce layer (2-layer cultural model). Project binds specific archetype overlay (e.g., Tionghoa Batam owner via `07-tionghoa-batam-overlay.md`).

### Register Table (WS1-REDO Indonesian low-arousal default)

| Layer | Register | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Owner (Tionghoa Batam archetype) — direct address via `{{owner_honorific}}`** | Calm, data-specific, practical, ROI-numerate, brief | *"`{{owner_honorific}}`, Anda hemat Rp X per bulan kalau solar leak Anda di-detect."* (NOT *"Transformasi digital fleet Anda!"*) |
| **Karyawan (Indonesian general) — sopir / admin / mekanik addressed** | Warm, face-saving, voice-note friendly, multi-ethnic safe | *"Konfirmasi pengisian solar tadi pagi ya, Pak Rohim."* (NOT *"Anda mencuri solar"*) |
| **Customer-facing (mixed)** | Professional + Batam-specific terminology accepted | *"Container Anda sudah sampai di Mukakuning, ETA Batamindo 15 menit."* |
| **VO casting (Speaker = INDUSIA)** | Neutral Indonesian, Batam-aware, NOT Jakarta-corporate, NOT Melayu-thick | Mid-tone male/female, conviction over enthusiasm |

### Honorific Asymmetry Rule (LOCK-5)

| Direction | Default Honorific | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Asisten / app / video VO → Pak Indra (owner) | **"Pak Tan" / "Cik" / "Koh"** (Hokkien-respectful, age-dependent) | Tionghoa Batam default (NOT "Pak Haji") |
| Pak Indra → sopir (downward, but respectful) | **"Pak Sopir Rohim"** atau **"Mas Hamdani"** | Preserve sopir dignity, multi-ethnic safe |
| Asisten → sopir (clarification mode) | **"Pak Rohim"** + face-saving framing | Anti-fraud-with-dignity workflow |
| Customer-facing VO → owner mentioned | `{{owner_honorific}}` atau **"Bapak"** generic | Default safe |

**Validation:** Persona answers may override default honorific (e.g., user confirms Pak Indra prefer "Pak Indra" over "Pak Tan"). Re-Edit `project_pak_indra_archetype.md` if so.

### Dual-Calendar Content Rule (LOCK-5)

| Layer | Calendar Awareness |
|---|---|
| Owner (Tionghoa) | **Imlek + Cap Go Meh + Qingming + Vesak** — content / promo align dengan calendar ini |
| Karyawan (Indonesian) | **Eid + Ramadhan + Christmas** — operations + recruitment + motivation video align |
| Cross-friction | Dual-holiday awareness — operations gap during Imlek (owner libur) + Eid (sopir libur) = no overlap, both must be planned |

### Anti-Fraud Framing Tension (LOCK-5 cross-layer)

Tionghoa owner direct on fraud detection ↔ Indonesian sopir face-saving. Brand voice rule:

| Output | Tone |
|---|---|
| Owner dashboard alert | Direct, data-specific: *"Anomali: B-5678 fuel gap 15L jam 9:15 di SPBU Mukakuning. Saya tahan invoice fuel B-5678?"* |
| Sopir-facing WhatsApp clarification | Face-saving, clarification framing: *"Pak Rohim, ada catatan pengisian solar tadi pagi. Bisa konfirmasi ya?"* |
| Public-facing video promo | Show owner-gets-data + sopir-gets-dignity simultaneously — NEVER frame sopir publicly as thief |
| Recruitment video | Frame anti-fraud as **fairness for honest sopir** — fraud detection protects honest sopir from being blamed |

### Speaker (Agency) Voice — AI + IoT + Mobile Solution

`{{agency_company}}` narrator/host voice = neutral Indonesian, regionally-aware (e.g., Batam-aware for Batam-logistik clients), register calibrated to `{{owner_register}}`. Triangulation rule:

- Every claim MUST link to 1 of 3 pillars: *"AI deteksi anomali (Pillar 1)"*, *"IoT sensor [domain] (Pillar 2)"*, *"Mobile dashboard di HP Bapak (Pillar 3)"*
- Differentiator phrasing: *"Bukan corporate big-data kayak [incumbent unnamed]. Tapi dashboard yang owner langsung pakai dari HP."* (avoid naming `{{competitor_incumbent}}` publicly)
- Anti-fluff: NEVER use "transformasi digital" / "revolusi industri 4.0" / "next-gen" / "AI-powered" jargon. Replace with concrete output ("Bapak tahu solar Anda menguap 15 liter, AI-nya yang detect.").
