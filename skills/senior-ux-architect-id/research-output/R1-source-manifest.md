## Notes

### Rejected Sources (with reason)
- Generic "top 10 UX tips" listicles (no author credential, AI-generated SEO farm pattern)
- ToptalDesigners blog on Cooper personas — covered better by direct Cooper book + Pruitt/Adlin
- Marketing-driven "Gen Z statistics" pages without methodology disclosure
- Pre-2020 Smashing/UXmatters articles where 2023+ updates exist
- Several Indonesian Medium case studies that were design-school portfolio pieces without empirical data
- BeReal "2026 statistics" pages from amraandelma.com (suspect numbers, no methodology)

### Gap Analysis
- **No first-party Grab driver UX teardown** — Grab engineering blog less public than Gojek's. Mitigated by IEEE comparative study (#48) + Indonesian academic comparison (#60).
- **Kids segment thinner than ideal (4 sources)** — Indonesian PAUD-specific research is sparse in English/Bahasa academic indexes. PBS + COPPA + ethical framework + Gapsy guide cover the core; project-specific Indonesian kids research should be commissioned per-engagement.
- **No dedicated "gaming-mainstream" Indonesian source** — Mobile Legends / Free Fire UX research underrepresented in academic indexes; consult community teardowns ad hoc per-project.
- **High-edu / professional B2C segment** is covered implicitly via enterprise B2B + canon + Cooper About Face (designed-for-IT-professionals patterns transfer well).

### Stretch Additions (recommended if budget allows next pass)
- GSMA Mobile Gender Gap Report — fills low-edu gender-overlap gap
- Universitas Indonesia HCI lab thesis collection — deeper Indonesian academic coverage
- Tokopedia tech blog (medium.com/tokopedia) — first-party Indonesian engineering UX writing
- Apple WWDC accessibility session videos — practical iOS accessibility implementation
- Stark + Contrast Checker tooling docs — bridge from guideline to verification

### Most Strategically Important (Top 5)
1. **NN/g UX Design for Seniors 3rd ed. (#28)** — only comprehensive empirical senior UX baseline; instantly justifies design choices for elderly segment
2. **Gojek UI/UX origin story (#47)** — irreplaceable first-party account of designing for blue-collar drivers in Indonesia; canonical reference for `senior-ux-architect-id`'s sopir/admin patterns
3. **WCAG 2.2 + WCAG2Mobile draft (#37, #38)** — current binding standard + fresh 2025 mobile-app applicability; must be the accessibility north star
4. **Mobile Money for Illiterate Users in Ethiopia (#11)** — proven icon-first patterns directly transferable to driver-app + field-worker app design for Indonesia
5. **APJII 2025 + DataReportal Digital 2025 Indonesia (#56, #57)** — current macro baseline (229M users, urban 85% vs rural 77%, low digital literacy) sets the context every Indonesian project starts from
