# 04 — Business Development & Go-To-Market Playbook

How to take a model to market and close. Grounded in `research-output/nlm-research-report.md` (Jimo, Salesmotion, Expando AI, Octave, GTMnow). Citations inline.

## 1. Choose the GTM motion (benchmarks, 2026)

| Metric | Product-Led (PLG) | Sales-Led (SLG) | Partner-Led |
|---|---|---|---|
| Median target ACV | < $10k | **~$25,000** | up to **+41%** larger |
| Free→paid conversion | ~9% | n/a | +28% more likely to buy |
| Target CAC | ~$8,000 | higher | **−60% vs direct** |
| Sales-cycle speed | very fast | slow (60–180 days) | **−46% vs direct** |
| Primary driver | self-activation | relationship/negotiation | 3rd-party trust |

*(per Jimo, Salesmotion, Expando AI)*

**Selection logic:**
- Low ACV, self-serve value, fast TTV → **PLG**.
- High ACV, multi-stakeholder, on-prem/industrial, procurement-heavy → **SLG**.
- An incumbent owns the customers/trust you need (e.g. {{incumbent_partner}}) → **Partner-Led** (cheaper CAC, faster cycle, bigger deals). For asset-rich-incumbent collaborations this is usually the lead motion — see `05`.
- Most B2B AI winners run a **hybrid**: PLG/pilot to land, SLG/partner to expand.

## 2. Agent-Led Growth (ALG) — the new onboarding edge

ALG = the agent *operates the product on the user's behalf* ("Self-Operate") instead of making the user learn it ("Self-Serve"). Drives free→paid conversion to **25–30%** vs **3–9%** for traditional PLG (per Jimo).
- **Paradox:** faster value but potentially *lower stickiness* — users skip the learning that builds attachment (per Jimo). Counter by embedding into workflow + data flywheel (moat, see `02`).

## 3. Land-and-expand

- **Land** = low-friction entry engineered to prove value fast: paid pilot, per-unit SKU, productized audit. Cheap to say yes to; covers setup/CAC.
- **Expand** = platform/recurring/outcome once value is proven. Use **PQLs** (Product-Qualified Leads — accounts showing high-intensity in-product behavior) to trigger the Sales-Led expansion at the right moment (per Jimo + Salesmotion).
- Engineer the entry SKU *for* expansion (pattern #45): the pilot's success metric should be the wedge into the recurring model.

## 4. AI Proof-of-Concept (PoC) framework

Modern PoCs optimize **Time-to-First-Value (TTV)**, not feature checklists.
- Define **one** success metric tied to the value metric (e.g. defect-escape rate, hours saved) *before* the pilot.
- Time-box (2–6 weeks), fixed scope, on the customer's real data.
- Pre-agree the **conversion trigger**: "if PoC hits X, it converts to {contract} at {price}." A PoC without a pre-negotiated expansion path is a science project.
- Capture data/behavioral signals during PoC → feeds both the expansion case and the moat.

## 5. Deal structuring & term-sheet logic

For any non-trivial deal (esp. partnerships, see `05`), make these explicit — vagueness here is where value leaks and disputes start (per Aluko & Oyebode, Jim Bergman):
- **Price & mechanism** — value metric, floors/caps, ramps, minimums.
- **Revenue split / margin** — exact %, by deal type; who bills the customer.
- **IP ownership** — Background vs Foreground vs Derivative/Improvement rights (see `05` §IP). Decisive.
- **Exclusivity** — scope (vertical/region/time), carve-outs, performance conditions to keep it.
- **Term, renewal, exit/transfer** — for BOT: the transfer trigger, price, and earn-out.
- **Incentives** — gainsharing, milestone-based payments, tiered partner rewards (Bronze/Gold) to drive sustained performance (per Jim Bergman).
- **Data rights** — who owns/accesses the data generated (feeds the moat; often more valuable than the fee).
- **Change management** — explicit plan to overcome legacy-system friction + staff education gap, the top adoption killers (per Jimo, Salesmotion).

## 6. Buyer psychology (esp. SME / industrial / SE-Asia)

- Practical-skeptic, capex-sensitive owners buy via a **paid pilot**, not a deck. De-risk the first yes.
- Match the **budget line**: opex buyer → subscription/usage; capex buyer → appliance/deploy purchase. Offer both where possible.
- Trust transfers through the channel — a partner-led motion with a respected incumbent compresses the trust-building cycle (the −46% cycle, −60% CAC advantage).
