# Comprehensive Study Guide: Financial Accounting Standards, Taxation, and Corporate Governance

This study guide provides an exhaustive synthesis of the transition from SAK ETAP to SAK EP in Indonesia, the mechanics of manufacturing financial reports, essential transaction documentation, and contemporary tax regulations under the Coretax system.

---

## 1. The Transition to SAK Entitas Privat (SAK EP)

Effective January 1, 2025 (with early adoption permitted since January 1, 2022), the Indonesian Accounting Standards Board (DSAK IAI) replaced SAK ETAP with **SAK Entitas Privat (SAK EP)**. This standard is aligned with the IFRS for SMEs, aimed at increasing transparency and accountability for private entities.

### Key Accounting Differences
The transition shifts reporting from a simplified historical cost basis toward more complex accrual and fair value assessments.

| Feature | SAK ETAP (Old) | SAK EP (New) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Revenue Recognition** | Often based on general estimates of completion. | Uses a rigorous **Five-Step Model**: Identify contract, performance obligations, transaction price, allocation, and recognition. |
| **Receivables** | Recognized at nominal value; provisions made only when clearly uncollectible. | Utilizes the **Expected Credit Loss (ECL)** model; requires aging schedules and discounting for long-term, interest-free receivables. |
| **Post-Employment Benefits** | Often optional or recognized on a cash basis. | **Mandatory** recognition of liabilities using actuarial methods or simplified estimates. |
| **Financial Instruments** | Recorded at historical cost. | Long-term debt (bank loans, leasing) measured at **amortized cost** using the Effective Interest Rate (EIR). |
| **Fixed Assets** | Historical cost minus depreciation. | Mandatory **Impairment Testing** (testing recoverable amount vs. book value). |
| **Investments** | Generally historical cost. | Requires the **Equity Method** for associates and joint ventures; includes annual impairment evaluations. |

### Implementation Challenges
1.  **Technical:** Shift from cash basis to accrual; complexity of the five-step revenue model.
2.  **Human Resources:** Limited accounting literacy and lack of staff competent in IFRS-based principles.
3.  **Data/System:** Limited historical data and the need for new software or internal training.
4.  **Cost:** Direct costs (training, software) and indirect costs (potential penalties for non-compliance).

## 2. Manufacturing Financial Reporting

Manufacturing accounting is more complex than trading because it involves the transformation of raw materials into finished goods.

### Types of Inventory
1.  **Raw Materials:** Unprocessed goods intended for production.
2.  **Work in Progress (WIP):** Products currently in the production phase.
3.  **Finished Goods:** Completed products ready for sale.

### Components of the Income Statement (Multi-Step)
*   **Revenue:** Total sales of finished goods.
*   **Cost of Goods Sold (COGS/HPP):** Sum of **Prime Costs** (Direct Materials + Direct Labor) and **Factory Overhead** (indirect costs like electricity, rent, and machine depreciation).
*   **Gross Profit:** Revenue minus COGS.
*   **Operating Expenses:** Selling expenses (marketing, commissions) and Administrative expenses (office rent, staff salaries).
*   **Operating Profit:** Gross Profit minus Operating Expenses.
*   **Net Profit:** Final profit after accounting for non-operating items and taxes.

## 3. Transaction Documentation and Proofs

Valid documentation serves as the foundation for audits, tax reporting, and legal compliance.

### Essential Transaction Proofs
*   **Cash In/Out:** Records of cash movements for journal entries.
*   **Receipt (Kwitansi):** Signed by the receiver; proves receipt of funds.
*   **Invoice (Faktur):** Detailed statement of goods sold; includes price, quantity, and payment terms.
*   **Debit/Credit Notes:** Used for returns or price adjustments due to damaged goods.
*   **Check/Bilyet Giro:** Orders for bank payments or account transfers.
*   **Memorandum:** Internal proof for non-cash events (e.g., month-end salary accruals).

### VAT Documents Equated to Tax Invoices
Under **PER-16/PJ/2021**, 25 types of documents are legally equated to a Tax Invoice (*Faktur Pajak*), including:
1.  **SPPB:** Issued by BULOG/DOLOG for flour distribution.
2.  **Telecom/Electricity/Water Bills:** Tagged as taxable proofs.
3.  **Tickets/Airway Bills:** For domestic air transport.
4.  **PIB (Export/Import Declaration):** Must include identity (NPWP/NIK) and payment proofs to be creditable.

## 4. Taxation: Compliance and the Coretax Era

### New Regulations (PER-11/PJ/2025)
The implementation of the Coretax system has introduced several changes:
*   **e-Faktur Deadline:** The upload deadline is extended from the 15th to the **20th** of the following month.
*   **Individual Withholding:** Individuals conducting business (freelancers/UMKMs) are now mandatory withholderes for rent payments.
*   **Simplified SPT Masa:** Consolidated into **SPT Masa PPh 21/26** and **SPT Masa PPh Unifikasi** (combining Articles 22, 23, 15, and 4(2)).

### Income Tax Article 23 (PPh 23)
Deducted from income sourced from capital, awards, or services (not already covered by PPh 21).

| Rate | Subject Matter |
| :--- | :--- |
| **15%** | Dividends, Interest, Royalties, and Awards/Bonuses. |
| **2%** | Rent of assets (except land/buildings), technical, management, consulting, and legal services. |

*Note: If the recipient does not have an NPWP, the rate is 100% higher (e.g., 2% becomes 4%).*

## 5. Corporate Governance and Ethics

### Managing Uncollectible Accounts (Bad Debt)
*   **Direct Write-Off Method:** Directly records losses; only suitable for immaterial amounts.
*   **Allowance Method:** Prefers for material amounts. Uses a contra-asset account to estimate losses based on:
    *   **Percentage of Sales:** Based on historical bad debt rates.
    *   **Percentage of Receivables:** Uses an **Aging Schedule** to estimate losses based on how long a debt has been outstanding.

### Internal Fraud and Embezzlement
Recent legal cases (e.g., PT Asta International and PT Cipta Niaga Semesta) highlight the dangers of inadequate internal controls. Cashiers in these entities embezzled funds (ranging from Rp 90 million to Rp 730 million) primarily to fund **online gambling**. Such crimes often result in prison sentences (2 to 3.5 years) and emphasize the need for regular audits and supervisor oversight.

### Tax Strategies: Planning vs. Evasion
*   **Tax Planning:** Legal and optimal strategies using existing laws (e.g., choosing a specific depreciation method).
*   **Tax Avoidance:** Legal but often considered unethical; utilizes "gray areas" or loopholes (e.g., transfer pricing).
*   **Tax Evasion:** **Illegal** acts to avoid tax (e.g., hiding income, forging documents, or manipulating financial statements).

## 6. Short-Answer Practice Questions

1.  **What is the primary difference between the treatment of bad debt under SAK ETAP and SAK EP?**
    *   *Answer:* SAK ETAP only recognizes bad debt when it is clearly uncollectible, whereas SAK EP requires the recognition of Expected Credit Losses (ECL) based on risk assessments and historical data.
2.  **Calculate the PPh 23 for a consulting service worth Rp 20,000,000 paid to a provider with an NPWP.**
    *   *Answer:* 2% x Rp 20,000,000 = Rp 400,000.
3.  **List the three main components of HPP (Cost of Goods Sold) in a manufacturing company.**
    *   *Answer:* Direct Materials (Raw Materials), Direct Labor, and Factory Overhead.
4.  **Under PER-11/PJ/2025, what is the new deadline for uploading an e-Faktur?**
    *   *Answer:* The 20th of the following month.
5.  **What is the tax rate for rent of land and buildings, and under which article does it fall?**
    *   *Answer:* 10% under PPh Final Article 4(2).

## 8. Glossary of Important Terms

*   **Amortized Cost:** The initial recognition amount minus principal repayments, plus/minus cumulative amortization using the effective interest method.
*   **Coretax:** The integrated core tax administration system used by the Indonesian Directorate General of Taxes for streamlined reporting.
*   **Expected Credit Loss (ECL):** An estimate of the probability of default on a receivable over its lifetime.
*   **Fiscal Reconciliation:** The process of adjusting commercial financial statements to comply with tax laws to determine taxable income.
*   **Impairment:** A reduction in the recoverable amount of a fixed asset below its current book value.
*   **Prime Cost:** The sum of direct material and direct labor costs in production.
*   **Tax Haven:** A country or jurisdiction with very low or no tax rates used to store profits legally (Tax Avoidance).
*   **Unifikasi (Unified SPT):** A single tax return used to report multiple types of income tax withholdings simultaneously.
