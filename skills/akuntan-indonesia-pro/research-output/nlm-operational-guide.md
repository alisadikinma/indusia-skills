# Operational Guide for the Precision-Driven Professional Accountant

## 1. The "Zero-Rupiah Margin" Mindset
In my 20 years as a Financial Controller, the transition from SAK ETAP to **SAK Entitas Privat (SAK EP)** marks the most significant shift in Indonesian accounting history. Effective January 1, 2025, we move from a simple historical cost model to a complex landscape of fair value, accrual-basis precision, and Expected Credit Loss (ECL).

In the era of the **Coretax System**, accuracy is no longer just a professional virtue; it is a survival requirement. Coretax allows the Directorate General of Taxes (DJP) to perform **automated real-time fiscal reconciliations**. If our internal SAK EP figures for "Impairment" or "ECL" do not align perfectly with our "Positive Fiscal Corrections," the system triggers an immediate Audit Flag. As a Precision-Driven Professional, your mindset must be: *Every entry is a legal testimony. Discrepancies are a failure of fiduciary oversight and must be escalated to the Audit Committee.*

## 2. Module 1: Daily Operational Precision Checklist [Printable]

**D.1 Physical Cash Count**
*   Conduct a daily reconciliation of "Kas Masuk" and "Kas Keluar" against the physical vault balance.
*   **Source Document:** Petty Cash Ledger & Daily Cash Count Sheet.
*   *Controller Alert:* A "Growing Vault Balance" that is not immediately transferred to the bank is a primary indicator of embezzlement (see PT Asta case).

**D.2 Bank Mutation Review**
*   Verify the "Rekening Koran" daily against internal "Bilyet Giro" or "Cek" registers.
*   Ensure every outgoing mutation is matched to an authorized payment voucher.
*   **Source Document:** Bank Statement (Online/E-Banking).

**D.3 Real-time Journal Posting**
*   Ensure all transaction evidence (Kwitansi, Nota Kontan, Faktur) is posted within the 24-hour cycle.
*   Categorize entries based on evidence type to facilitate month-end multi-step reporting.

**D.4 Document Validation (PER-16/PJ/2021)**
*   Apply "Syarat Formal" validation on all invoices (Name, NIK/NPWP, Address, DPP, PPN).
*   **Critical Update:** Recognize all **25 types** of "Dokumen yang Dipersamakan dengan Faktur Pajak" under PER-16/PJ/2021, including airway bills, electricity bills, and bank trading confirmations.

## 3. Module 2: Weekly Closing Routine [Weekly Control]

**W.1 Subledger Reconciliation (AR/AP)**
*   Reconcile AR/AP subledgers to the General Ledger. Any variance must be investigated immediately—do not wait for the monthly close.

**W.2 Return Verification (Nota Kredit/Debet)**
*   Audit all "Nota Kredit" (Sales Returns) and "Nota Debet" (Purchase Returns).
*   Cross-check against physical warehouse receiving slips to ensure returns are not fictitious.

**W.3 Cash Advance Aging**
*   Review employee "Cash Advances." Items older than 30 days are red flags. Settlement delays are often used to mask personal liquidity crises or "Judi Online" habits.

**W.4 e-Faktur Preparation (PER-11/PJ/2025)**
*   Prepare VAT data for upload.
*   **Regulatory Anchor:** Per PER-11/PJ/2025, the upload deadline is extended to the **20th of the following month**. Use this window for meticulous data scrubbing.

**W.5 Weekly Cash Flow Reporting**
*   Generate a "Single-step" logic report for owner review.
*   **Formula:** Total Cash Inflow - Total Cash Outflow = Net Cash Position. This provides a raw, unfiltered view of weekly liquidity.

## 4. Module 3: The 12-Step Monthly Closing Procedure (SAK EP Integrated)

**M.1 Bank Reconciliation**
*   Match "Setoran Bank" with internal ledgers. Resolve "Outstanding Checks" or "Deposits in Transit."

**M.2 AR Aging & ECL Calculation**
*   **Formula:** Saldo Piutang x % Kerugian Historis.
*   Identify receivables 90+ days old for specific impairment under SAK EP Bab 11.

**M.3 AP Aging & Loan Amortization**
*   Amortize "Utang Bank" and "Utang Leasing" using the **Effective Interest Rate (EIR)** method. Do not use flat nominal interest.

**M.4 Stock Opname & Laba Produksi**
*   Reconcile Raw Materials, WIP, and Finished Goods.
*   **Accounting Entry for Markup:** If transferring from factory to warehouse above cost:
    *   *Debit: Manufacturing Account*
    *   *Credit: P&L Account (Laba Produksi)*

**M.5 Fixed Asset Impairment Test**
*   Compare "Nilai Buku" (Book Value) vs. "Nilai Terpulihkan" (Recoverable Amount). Recognize loss if physical/economic obsolescence is detected.

**M.6 Related Party Loan Discounting**
*   Calculate Present Value (PV) for interest-free shareholder loans.
*   *Example (Rp3 Billion Loan, 3 Years, 8% Market Rate):* PV = Rp2,381,500,000.
*   **Transition Journal:**
    *   *Debit: Laba Ditahan Rp618,500,000*
    *   *Credit: Piutang Pemegang Saham Rp618,500,000*

**M.7 Revenue Recognition**
*   Apply the 5-step model (Bab 23 SAK EP). Identify "Kewajiban Kinerja" and defer revenue for unsatisfied performance obligations.

**M.8 Post-Employment Benefits**
*   Estimate "Imbalan Pascakerja" using: *Salary x Tenure x PV Factor*.

**M.9 Tax Calculation (PPh 21/23/PPN)**
*   Apply 2% for services and 15% for dividends/interest.

**M.10 Fiscal Reconciliation**
*   **Positive Corrections:** ECL, Impairment, and Post-Employment Benefit estimates (deductible only when paid/funded per UU PPh).

**M.11 Deferred Tax Recognition**
*   Identify "Pajak Tangguhan" from temporary differences identified in M.10.

**M.12 SPT Masa Generation**
*   Finalize and file via the "SPT Masa Unifikasi" format.

## 5. Module 4: Yearly Closing & SPT Tahunan

In the Coretax era, the DJP requires deep transparency.

**Mandatory Coretax Attachments:**
*   **Transfer Pricing (TP Doc):** Required for all affiliated transactions.
*   **Depreciation/Amortization Schedules:** Reconciled between SAK EP and Fiscal rates.
*   **Daftar Kepemilikan Saham:** Accurate record of all shareholders.

**Definitions of Integrity:**
*   **Tax Planning:** Legal optimization using government-sanctioned incentives (e.g., choice of depreciation).
*   **Tax Avoidance:** Exploiting "gray areas" or loopholes; carries high risk of sengketa pajak.
*   **Tax Evasion:** Illegal fraud, including hiding income or fictitious invoices.

## 6. Module 5: The "Red Flag" Detector (Audit Preparation)

| Risk Pattern | Indicator | Potential Issue |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Round Numbers** | Repeated journals ending in "000,000" | Potential fabricated estimates or non-evidence entries. |
| **Vault Balance Growth** | Cash balance grows while mutation frequency drops | Cash is being skimmed and not deposited (PT Asta case). |
| **Month-End Spikes** | Large journals on the 30th/31st without "Memorandum" | Attempt to hit targets via fictitious revenue. |
| **E-Faktur Mismatch** | Gap between physical invoices and e-Faktur portal | Unrecorded sales or "Faktur Fiktif" purchases. |
| **Settlement Delays** | Cash Advance > 30 days | Potential embezzlement for personal use (e.g., "Judi Online"). |

## 7. Module 6: Junior Accountant Coaching: Common Errors

*   **Capex vs. Opex:** Major overhauls that extend asset life must be capitalized, not expensed as maintenance.
*   **Fiscal Correction Omissions:** ECL and Impairment are **non-deductible**. Forgetting this leads to underpayment and penalties.
*   **Tax Rate Errors (No NPWP):** Under Coretax NIK-NPWP integration, vendors without NPWP are automatically flagged for a **100% penalty rate** (double the standard rate).
*   **Double Posting:** Implement a "Posted" stamp protocol for all physical receipts to prevent duplicate entries.

## 8. Module 7: Professional Communication Strategies

**Delivering Red Flags (Fact -> Impact -> Recommendation)**
*   *Script:* "Sir, we have identified that the vault balance has grown to Rp730 million without being deposited to the bank (Fact). This indicates a critical failure in internal control and mirrors the PT Asta case, which resulted in a **3.5-year prison term** for the staff involved (Impact). I recommend an immediate independent audit and the implementation of a dual-custody vault protocol (Recommendation)."

**Arguing Against Tax Aggression**
*   *Script:* "Reducing the tax bill via unrecorded sales is **Tax Evasion**. Under the current Coretax system, the DJP has real-time visibility. Hiding even Rp90 million—as seen in the PT Cipta Niaga case—led to a **2-year prison sentence**. Let us pivot to **Tax Planning** using legal depreciation methods to minimize your liability safely."
